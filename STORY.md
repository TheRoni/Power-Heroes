# Power Heroes: Story Bible

The full story and lore. `CLAUDE.md` has the short version; this file has the
details. Writing rule: short, warm, a bit funny. The biggest moments are quiet:
a few plain words, never a speech.

## The world
- **The Sky Isles** float on the **Heartlight**, a giant crystal at the centre
  of the world. Its light flows through every isle as colour.
- **Power is everything here.** Every living thing has a Power number, and you
  can see it as an aura. Kids compare auras. Clans post **Power Rankings**.
  Heroes enter the **Trials**. Everyone dreams of **Awakening**. Nobody thinks
  chasing Power is wrong, and mostly it is wonderful.
- **The Glooms** drain colour from the isles. A grey isle loses its light and
  slowly sinks. The stolen colour goes to someone.

## Theme
Everyone chases Power: you, your friends, your mentor, the villain. The game
celebrates that hunger; it never lectures. The story asks one question:
**what is your Power for?**
- Lyra chased it alone, and it took her memory and her name.
- Marlo gave his away to hold the isles up, and lost his daughter.
- You find the third way: Power grows biggest when it is shared.

## Characters
**You: the Newcomer** (default name **Sol**; the player can rename later).
Light type. You fall from the sky as a star, with no memory. Your Power starts
at 1 and climbs faster than anyone has ever seen. Eager, curious, hungry to
grow. *Secret (Chapter 5):* you are a shard of the Heartlight, split off when
Nyx struck it. That is why your Power has no known limit, and why Nyx hunts you.

**Keeper Marlo: your mentor.** Old, tall and broad, white beard, a grey-blue
cloak, a staff with a lantern that never goes out. Once the strongest hero
alive: his Power still shows as a faded **9,999**. He poured almost all of it
into the Heartlight to keep the isles afloat. Patient, dry, kind, a little
sad. He teaches you the game, one lesson per mechanic. At night he plays a
wooden flute: a lullaby he wrote for his daughter, who vanished years ago.
He is still searching for her. *He dies in Chapter 4.*
- "Everyone here wants more. So did I. So did she."

**Lyra, who became Nyx: the villain.** Marlo's daughter and the first Light
hero. Brilliant, proud, desperate to be stronger than her father. She went
into the Rift alone to break the limit. The Rift turned her Light to Shadow
and took her memory and her name. Now she is **Nyx, Queen of the Glooms**, and
the stolen colour feeds her. She believes strength is the only thing that
matters and the isles are weak. She hums a tune she cannot place: the lullaby.
- "Give me your light, little star. I'll put it to better use."

**Pip: the guide.** A tiny glowing light-sprite, comic relief and teacher's
helper. Pip was Lyra's companion when she was little, and lost most of their
glow the day she vanished. Pip stays with Marlo and avoids the subject. Pip
has no pronoun in the text.

**Ember (Fire).** The brightest young hero in Sunmeadow, aiming for number 1
on the Power Rankings, until a falling star outshines her on day one.
Hot-headed, competitive, fiercely loyal. Rival first, best friend later.

**Tide (Water).** From Tidecove, the isle that is sinking fastest. She chases
Power to save her home. Calm, dry humour, the squad's steady heart.

**Volt (Storm).** Obsessed with records: fastest dash, fastest Awakening, most
Glooms in one zap. Measures everything, talks fast, jokes faster.

**Rook (Storm).** Marlo's former student and number 1 on the Rankings when you
arrive. His power, Copycat, copies other heroes: he never found his own. He
resents that Marlo now trains you. You save him in Chapter 3, and he finally
finds a power that is his.

**The Generals** (Bramble, Undertow, Static, Scorch). Heroes who followed Nyx
into the Rift for more Power and came back hollow. When you beat one, they
are themselves again for a moment: one line of thanks, then they fade.
- "She promised us more. We just wanted to matter."

**The Colourfolk and the clan.** Villagers who cheer from the arena edge,
throw you fruit, ask to spar, and post your rise on the Rankings board.

## The arc
**Prologue: The Falling Star** (the tutorial). Night. A star falls into
Sunmeadow. You wake in a crater, glowing. A lantern bobs closer: Marlo, with
Pip. "Power 1. ...No. Look at it climb." Marlo teaches you to move, strike, and
use your power on a few Gloomlings. "Everyone in the Sky Isles chases Power.
Most never ask what it's for."

**Chapter 1: Sunmeadow** (Nature isle). Ember shows up, annoyed that a
newcomer is the talk of the isle: "Power 120. You?" You fight side by side.
Boss: General Bramble. That night at the campfire Marlo plays his flute and
tells you about Lyra. "She used to fall asleep before the second verse.
Every time." *Memory shard:* little Lyra on her father's shoulders.

**Chapter 2: Tidecove** (Water isle). The isle is tilting into the sea. Tide
joins to save it. Rook arrives, number 1 and smug, and races you to every
Gloom. Boss: General Undertow. Clue: the Glooms wear a crest, a crescent
around a star. Marlo goes very quiet. *Shard:* Lyra's first aura.

**Chapter 3: Stormspire** (Storm isle). Volt joins. Rook goes after the
General alone to prove himself and is overwhelmed. You save him; he joins.
Boss: General Static. Your Power is climbing frighteningly fast. Marlo trains
you harder. "Don't go where she went." *Shard:* Lyra arguing with her father.

**Chapter 4: Cinderpeak** (Fire isle). Nyx appears in person for the first
time, humming the lullaby. Pip freezes: they know that tune. She wants your
light. Marlo steps between you, looks into her face, and knows her. "Lyra?"
She does not know him. He pours the last of his Power into a shield to save
you, and fades. His last words, to you: "Tell her... the song was always
hers." Silence, a grey sky, then a sad flute. He leaves you his lantern and
flute, and the lantern Awakens your power for the first time.
Boss: General Scorch. *Shard:* Lyra leaving home at night.

**Chapter 5: The Heartlight.** The truth: you are a shard of the Heartlight,
and Nyx wants to absorb you to have Power with no limit. The final fight.
At its heart you play Marlo's flute and show her the memory shards. She
remembers. "...Dad?" The Shadow breaks; she holds his lantern and cries.
Then everyone (you, the squad, the clan, even Lyra) pours their Power into the
Heartlight together, and every isle blooms at once.

**Epilogue.** A memorial on Sunmeadow. Lyra plays the lullaby on her father's
flute, and for the first time someone finishes the second verse. She joins
the squad (Light and Shadow, both).

**After the story: The Rift.** An endless mode. Lyra knows the way in.

## Clues to plant early (and pay off later)
- The lullaby: Marlo plays it (Ch1), Nyx hums it (Ch4), Lyra finishes it (Epilogue).
- The crest, a crescent around a star: on the Glooms (Ch2), on Marlo's old
  cloak pin (Ch3), on Lyra's childhood shard (Ch4).
- Pip's dim glow, which brightens again when Lyra remembers.
- Marlo's faded "9,999", which reaches zero when he dies.
- The Generals' last words, all about wanting more.

## Music (all made in code, see CLAUDE.md)
The soundtrack is built around a **flute**. One melody, **Lyra's Lullaby**,
runs through the whole game in different moods.

| Cue | When | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| The Falling Star | prologue, title | slow, wondering flute, soft chimes |
| Sunmeadow | exploring, story scenes in Ch1 | bright, playful flute, light plucks |
| Battle | waves | quicker flute over soft drums |
| Marlo's Theme | lessons, campfire | warm, low flute |
| Lyra's Lullaby | campfire, shards | gentle solo flute |
| Lullaby, sorrow | Marlo's death, memories | very slow, minor, solo flute, long silences |
| Nyx | Nyx appears | low, breathy flute over a dark drone |
| Bloom | a chapter won, the finale | the lullaby in full, bright and big |
